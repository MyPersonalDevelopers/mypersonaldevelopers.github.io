Privacy notice

Developer and scope

The developer identified on the legal information page publishes this app and is responsible for the processing described here that the developer determines. Contact for support and privacy requests: MyPersonalDevelopers@gmail.com. This notice covers the current free app and its companion features where available, and the support website. It is a notice of actual practices, not blanket consent to new uses of data.

Camera, film rolls and photos

Plenka uses camera access to capture frames, apply film-style processing on the device and store rolls, image files, development status and app settings locally. The app does not send your camera feed or photographs to a developer server and does not run face recognition for identifying you. It does not require a developer-hosted account. No app-operated photo cloud or remote image-processing service is included in the inspected source.

Photos permission, sharing and notifications

Saving a developed roll uses Apple Photos and may create a separate album; this implementation requests Photos read/write authorization for that workflow. Notifications can announce that development is complete, if you allow them. Choosing Share hands the selected images to the app or recipient you choose. Apple Photos, iCloud Photos, device backups or a chosen sharing service may then store or transfer those copies under their own settings. This is distinct from Plenka uploading photos to the developer.

Managing and deleting Plenka data

Delete a film roll in Plenka to remove its local entry and associated app image files. Images already saved into Apple Photos or shared elsewhere are separate copies and are not deleted by removing a roll. Manage those copies in Photos, its Recently Deleted album and the destination services. Revoke Camera, Photos or notification access through system settings at any time. Removing the app normally removes its app-container data, but does not remove Photos images, user-saved exports, system backups or iCloud Photos copies. The developer cannot remotely access or delete your images.

Advertising, tracking and payments

The current free version has no advertising, advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking SDKs, developer-operated analytics SDKs, paid subscriptions or in-app purchases. The developer does not sell personal data or share it for targeted advertising. Future paid features or different data uses are not authorized by this notice: relevant disclosures and any legally required notices or consent must be provided before they start.

Support correspondence

If you email MyPersonalDevelopers@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, the information in your message and any attachments you choose to send. This is used to reply, investigate the reported issue, protect users and handle privacy requests. The mailbox uses Gmail, supplied by Google; email providers process delivery and mailbox information under their terms. See Google Privacy Policy. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, medical records, full university correspondence or other people’s personal data. Redact screenshots and logs before sending them. Email is optional; no support form or email content is collected by this website.

Website hosting

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no developer-added cookies, advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts, embedded videos, forms or user accounts. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security when serving Pages, even without a GitHub account; other connection information is handled under its privacy statement. See GitHub Pages: data collection and GitHub Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive an individual visitor log through this website. Following an external link or sending an email uses the destination provider’s services.

Apple services and diagnostics

Apple operates the App Store, operating-system permissions, device backup and Apple services used by the app. If you enable sharing with Apple or developers, Apple may provide crash or app-usage reports through its developer services. The developer may use reports made available by Apple to diagnose faults and improve reliability; no separate automatic reporting SDK is included. Apple’s own collection and retention are described in Apple Privacy Policy. System backups, iCloud Photos and user-selected Files providers may hold copies independently of the app.

Data is used to provide the features you request, store your choices, respond to support, secure the service and meet applicable legal obligations. Where GDPR or a similar law applies, ordinary processing necessary to provide a requested service relies on contractual necessity; optional processing relies on consent where required; limited support and security processing may rely on legitimate interests in resolving requests and protecting users, subject to your rights; legally required records rely on the relevant legal obligation. Health or other specially protected information requires an applicable additional legal condition where that law requires one. A system permission is a device control and must not be treated as unrestricted legal consent. You can refuse optional access and revoke it in system settings; the corresponding feature may then stop working. There is no developer-operated automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

Retention, recipients and international processing

Local app content is retained while needed for the feature and until you delete it, subject to the specific controls below. Support messages are kept only while necessary to resolve the request and follow-up, maintain security or meet a specific legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim; messages no longer needed for those purposes should be deleted. You can request earlier deletion, subject to applicable legal exceptions. There is no fixed universal retention period for data held independently by Apple, Google, GitHub or a service you choose. These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States; their policies explain their processing locations, retention and transfer arrangements. Their use does not establish that every international transfer requirement has been met. Information is disclosed to service providers as needed for the described purposes, to recipients you select, or where legally required; no app content is routinely uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Your rights and how to contact us

Depending on the law that applies, you may request information about processing, access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. Write to MyPersonalDevelopers@gmail.com and identify the app and request; provide only what is reasonably needed to verify the request. We handle requests within applicable legal deadlines, with any lawful exceptions or extensions explained. You may complain to your competent data protection authority, including Roskomnadzor in Russia, an EEA supervisory authority or the UK ICO where applicable. The developer cannot remotely inspect or erase data held only on your device or independently controlled by a university or another provider; we can explain the available controls or direct you to the relevant provider. Nothing here limits mandatory statutory rights.

The App Store age rating describes content; it is not a substitute for any legally required parental consent or service eligibility rules. Камера, плёнки и фотографии

Plenka использует камеру для съёмки кадров, применяет плёночную обработку на устройстве и локально хранит плёнки, файлы изображений, состояние проявки и настройки. Приложение не отправляет видеопоток камеры и фотографии на сервер разработчика и не распознаёт лица для установления личности. Аккаунт на сервере разработчика не требуется. В проверенных исходниках нет собственного облака фотографий или внешнего сервиса обработки изображений.

Фототека, отправка и уведомления

Сохранение проявленной плёнки использует Apple Photos и может создавать отдельный альбом; для этой функции текущая реализация запрашивает чтение и запись фототеки. С вашего разрешения уведомления сообщают об окончании проявки. Действие «Поделиться» передаёт выбранные изображения указанному вами приложению или получателю. Apple Photos, «Фото iCloud», резервное копирование устройства и выбранный сервис отправки могут затем хранить и передавать эти копии по своим настройкам. Это отличается от загрузки фотографий разработчику через Plenka.

Управление и удаление данных Plenka

Удаление плёнки в Plenka убирает её локальную запись и связанные файлы изображений приложения. Сохранённые в Apple Photos и отправленные в другие сервисы изображения являются отдельными копиями и не удаляются вместе с плёнкой. Управляйте ими в «Фото», альбоме «Недавно удалённые» и соответствующих сервисах. Доступ к камере, фототеке и уведомлениям можно отозвать в настройках системы. Удаление приложения обычно удаляет данные его контейнера, но не изображения в «Фото», сохранённые экспорты, системные резервные копии и «Фото iCloud». Разработчик не имеет удалённого доступа к вашим снимкам и не может стереть их.
