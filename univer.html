Privacy notice

Developer and scope

Gorshkov Daniil Dmitrievich, an individual developer based in Russia, publishes this app and is responsible for the processing described here that the developer determines. Contact for support and privacy requests: MyPersonalDevelopers@gmail.com. This notice covers the current free app and its companion features where available, and the support website. It is a notice of actual practices, not blanket consent to new uses of data.

University services and account data

Univer is an independently developed client for SPbSUT (Bonch-Bruevich Saint Petersburg State University of Telecommunications) services. It is not presented as an official university app and does not act on behalf of the university. Public timetables and staff directory information, including names, work contacts and photographs supplied by university pages where available, are requested from www.sut.ru and lk.sut.ru. Sign-in, grades, attendance, profile information, messages, recipients and attachments use the university personal-account service directly from your device. A public timetable can be used without entering university credentials. Private academic and messaging features require an authorized university account.

Credentials, messages and local storage

Login and password are used to sign in to the university service and are stored using the device Keychain; the inspected credential vault does not enable Keychain iCloud synchronization. The university receives sign-in requests and actions you perform, including messages and attachments sent to their recipients. The app stores academic information, messages, downloaded files, homework, teacher notes, settings and limited diagnostic history locally to support its features and offline use. Developer-operated servers do not receive university passwords or the contents of your academic account. This does not prevent the university, a selected recipient or a device you pair from receiving the information needed for the action you request.

iCloud, device pairing and permissions

Some portable preferences, including selected teachers and notification/interface choices, use Apple iCloud key-value storage. Optional homework synchronization uses the user’s private CloudKit database and can include assignment text and attachments; it is controlled separately in the app. Disabling homework synchronization does not erase earlier cloud copies and does not disable the separate preference-storage mechanism. Local QR pairing can transfer an encrypted package containing university login, password and profile to a Mac so that the Mac can sign in independently. Confirm pairing only with a device you trust; a QR image alone must not be described as containing all credentials. Camera, selected photos/files, local network, notifications, alarms and biometric protection are used for their available user-requested features. Biometric matching is performed by the operating system; the developer does not receive a fingerprint or face template. Widgets and Live Activities may show schedule or attendance information on system surfaces; device notification and lock-screen settings control their visibility.

Managing and deleting Univer data

Signing out is not an erase-all function: an offline library can remain. The offline-data clearing function removes the caches it covers; it is not a promise to erase homework, all Keychain entries, paired Mac copies or iCloud records. Downloaded or shared files may also exist outside the app. Removing the app does not delete the university account, university records, recipients’ messages, other devices’ copies or all Keychain/iCloud data. Manage permission and notification access in system settings. Where iCloud storage controls expose this app’s data, use those controls to manage cloud copies; contact support for assistance if a record cannot be removed. The developer has no administrative access to the university account or the user’s private iCloud database. Requests to correct or delete university records must go to the university. Do not use auto-attendance or background features in ways inconsistent with university rules.

Advertising, tracking and payments

The current free version has no advertising, advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking SDKs, developer-operated analytics SDKs, paid subscriptions or in-app purchases. The developer does not sell personal data or share it for targeted advertising. Future paid features or different data uses are not authorized by this notice: relevant disclosures and any legally required notices or consent must be provided before they start.
